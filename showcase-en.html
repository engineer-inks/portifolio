Problem vs. Solution

03 / 13

IFRS 9 Risk Engine

The Problem

Training IFRS 9 models (PD, LGD, EAD) requires massive datasets, often restricted by privacy regulations (GDPR/LGPD) or default data scarcity.

The Solution

Development of a synthetic generator using Gaussian Copulas and Monte Carlo Simulation to create profiles while preserving non-linear dependencies.

Financial Foundation

04 / 13

Expected Loss

The pillar of modern credit risk under the IFRS 9 framework. The modeling complies with the banking provision equation:

$$EL = PD \times LGD \times EAD$$

Statistical Theory

05 / 13

Sklar's Theorem

The "master key" that allows us to separate individual marginal distributions from their joint correlation structure:

$$H(x_1, \dots, x_d) = C(F_1(x_1), \dots, F_d(x_d))$$

Dependency Modeling

06 / 13

Gaussian Copulas

Maps uniform variables to the multivariate normal space, injecting the correlation matrix $R$:

$$C_R(u_1, \dots, u_d) = \Phi_R(\Phi^{-1}(u_1), \dots, \Phi^{-1}(u_d))$$

Generative Algorithm

07 / 13

Monte Carlo Process

1. Sampling

Independent random vector $\epsilon \sim \mathcal{N}(0, I)$

2. Cholesky

Dependency injection: $Z = L \cdot \epsilon$

3. Inversion

Real mapping: $X_i = F_i^{-1}(\Phi(Z_i))$
