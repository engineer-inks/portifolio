IFRS 9 Risk Engine & Synthetic Data

Credit Risk / Machine Learning

Architecture for generating synthetic financial data while preserving complex dependency structures between credit variables. This solution enables the training of predictive models (PD, LGD, EAD) without violating sensitive data privacy or facing historical sampling bottlenecks.

Mathematical Foundations

  • Gaussian Copulas and correlation matrices
  • Sklar's Theorem for non-parametric marginals
  • Monte Carlo Simulation via Cholesky Decomposition

Data Engineering & Cloud

  • Unified pipeline in Python and PySpark (Lakehouse)
  • Structured data in columnar format (Parquet)
  • Ready for Cloud Serverless integration
