About Me

Machine Learning Engineer & Data Scientist focused on transforming applied mathematics and cloud data architectures into business intelligence.

Background & Experience

I am a Software Developer and Machine Learning Engineer with solid experience in developing Artificial Intelligence solutions, Data Engineering, and mathematical modeling applied to the financial sector — with a strong emphasis on Credit Risk.

With practical experience in the banking sector and large technology initiatives, I work on building and maintaining robust data pipelines, integrating advanced predictive models (such as CatBoost and PyTorch), and orchestrating scalable processes in the cloud (GCP). I have a strong focus on solving complex business problems, covering IFRS 9 regulations, default forecasting, and statistical risk simulation.

Beyond the financial ecosystem and tabular data, I have a strong technical background in unstructured data, working with Natural Language Processing (NLP), audio processing, and Speech-to-Text models at innovative companies such as YH Brasil, Myra, Mutant, and MedRoom.

Always aligned with software engineering best practices (WSL, Docker, Git) and information security, my focus is on delivering auditable, efficient, and highly strategic value.
